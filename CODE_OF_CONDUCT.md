# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple standard: keep technical discussion respectful,
specific, and useful.

Harassment, personal attacks, hate speech, sexualized harassment, threats, and
deliberate disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions,
or other project spaces.

When disagreements happen, focus on the code, the user impact, the evidence,
and the tradeoffs. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block
participants who make the project unsafe or unproductive for others.
